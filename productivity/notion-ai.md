# Notion AI

> AI built into Notion — summarizes pages, drafts docs, autofills tables, and answers questions from your workspace.

**Category:** Productivity · **Official site:** https://www.notion.so/product/ai

## What it is
AI built into Notion — summarizes pages, drafts docs, autofills tables, and answers questions from your workspace.

## Best for
Summarizing meeting notes, drafting docs from bullets, turning messy pages into tables, workspace Q&A.

## Pricing
Add-on ~$10/member/mo on top of Notion; bundled in some business plans.

## How to use it
1. Inside any Notion page, press Space or type '/ai'.
2. Highlight text and ask for summarize / improve / translate.
3. Use Q&A to ask questions across your whole workspace.

## Prompting tips
- Feed it your own pages as context: 'turn these meeting notes into action items with owners.'
- Custom AI blocks can autofill database properties at scale.

## Gotchas
- Only as good as your Notion organization — messy workspace, messy answers.
- It's an add-on cost, not included in base Notion plans.
