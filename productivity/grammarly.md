# Grammarly

> AI writing assistant that fixes grammar, sharpens tone, and now rewrites with generative AI — everywhere you type.

**Category:** Productivity · **Official site:** https://www.grammarly.com

## What it is
AI writing assistant that fixes grammar, sharpens tone, and now rewrites with generative AI — everywhere you type.

## Best for
Error-free emails, tone adjustment, clearer writing across every app.

## Pricing
Free tier covers basics; Premium (~$12/mo) adds tone, clarity, and generative rewrites.

## How to use it
1. Install the browser extension and desktop/mobile apps.
2. It underlines issues as you type — click for fixes.
3. Use the generative AI prompt bar for rewrites: 'make this more confident.'

## Prompting tips
- Set your audience and formality in settings so suggestions match your context.
- The tone detector before sending important emails is genuinely useful.

## Gotchas
- Free tier nags you about Premium constantly.
- Don't accept every suggestion blindly — it can flatten your personal voice.
