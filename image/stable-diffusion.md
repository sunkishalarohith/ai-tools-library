# Stable Diffusion

> Open-source image generation you can run on your own computer.

**Category:** Image generation · **Official site:** https://stability.ai

## What it is
Open-source image generation you can run on your own computer. Free, private, endlessly customizable with community models.

## Best for
Free unlimited generation, privacy (nothing leaves your machine), custom styles via community models, NSFW-free control of your own pipeline.

## Pricing
Free and open source; you supply the computer (a decent GPU helps a lot).

## How to use it
1. Easiest start: download a one-click package like Stability Matrix or Fooocus.
2. Pick a checkpoint model matching your style (realistic, anime, etc.) from Civitai.
3. Prompt, generate, adjust settings (steps, CFG scale), repeat.

## Prompting tips
- Learn the two knobs first: steps (quality, 20-30 is plenty) and CFG scale (prompt adherence, ~7).
- Negative prompts ('blurry, deformed, watermark') fix common artifacts.

## Gotchas
- Setup is technical — expect an afternoon of tinkering the first time.
- Needs a real GPU for reasonable speed; CPU generation is painfully slow.
