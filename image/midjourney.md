# Midjourney

> The highest-quality AI image generator for artistic, stylized, photorealistic images.

**Category:** Image generation · **Official site:** https://www.midjourney.com

## What it is
The highest-quality AI image generator for artistic, stylized, photorealistic images. Runs through Discord or its web app.

## Best for
Stunning artwork, marketing visuals, concept art, photorealistic scenes with mood and style.

## Pricing
No free tier; plans from ~$10/mo (limited generations) up.

## How to use it
1. Join via midjourney.com, subscribe, then prompt in the web app or Discord.
2. Basic prompt: '/imagine a cozy bookstore at night, warm lighting, cinematic --ar 16:9'.
3. Use --ar for aspect ratio (16:9, 1:1, 9:16); --style raw for less beautified output.

## Prompting tips
- Style words matter more than detail: 'cinematic lighting, 35mm, moody' beats a paragraph of description.
- Generate 4, pick the best, then use Vary/Remix to iterate — don't try to nail it in one prompt.
- For consistent characters, use the character-reference (--cref) feature with an image URL.

## Gotchas
- No free tier — every generation costs credits.
- Text in images is still unreliable; hands and fingers can look wrong.
