# DeepSeek

> A free, open-weights AI chatbot from China that matches paid models on reasoning, math, and coding benchmarks.

**Category:** Chat / LLMs · **Official site:** https://chat.deepseek.com

## What it is
A free, open-weights AI chatbot from China that matches paid models on reasoning, math, and coding benchmarks.

## Best for
Free high-quality reasoning, math problem-solving, coding help — without a subscription.

## Pricing
Free to use; API is extremely cheap compared to competitors.

## How to use it
1. Sign up at chat.deepseek.com — no payment needed.
2. Turn on 'DeepThink' (R1) for hard reasoning problems; it shows its step-by-step thinking.
3. Use it like ChatGPT for general questions.

## Prompting tips
- For logic/math puzzles, explicitly use the reasoning mode and ask it to verify its own answer.
- Great free option for students and anyone avoiding subscriptions.

## Gotchas
- Data is handled under Chinese jurisdiction — avoid sensitive personal or work data.
- Occasionally slower during peak hours.
