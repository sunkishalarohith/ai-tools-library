# Claude

> Anthropic's AI assistant, known for careful reasoning, strong writing, and long-document handling.

**Category:** Chat / LLMs · **Official site:** https://claude.ai

## What it is
Anthropic's AI assistant, known for careful reasoning, strong writing, and long-document handling. Excels at nuanced writing, analysis, and coding.

## Best for
Long documents and reports, careful writing and editing, coding, complex reasoning, research summaries.

## Pricing
Free tier with daily limits; Pro (~$20/mo) for higher limits and newest models.

## How to use it
1. Sign up at claude.ai (web, iOS, Android).
2. Paste or upload long documents — Claude handles very long context well.
3. Use Projects to store background knowledge it should reuse across chats.
4. Try Artifacts for code, documents, and designs it builds for you in a side panel.

## Prompting tips
- Claude responds well to explicit style instructions: 'write in short paragraphs, no jargon.'
- For analysis, ask for structure: 'give me the three strongest arguments for and against.'
- Upload a writing sample and say 'match this tone' for consistent voice.

## Gotchas
- Daily message limits on the free tier can run out mid-task.
- Like all LLMs, it can hallucinate — double-check citations and facts.
