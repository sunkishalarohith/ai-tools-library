# ChatGPT

> OpenAI's conversational AI assistant.

**Category:** Chat / LLMs · **Official site:** https://chat.openai.com

## What it is
OpenAI's conversational AI assistant. The most widely used AI chatbot — answers questions, writes and edits text, codes, analyzes files and images, and browses the web.

## Best for
General questions, writing help, coding assistance, brainstorming, learning new topics, data analysis.

## Pricing
Free tier with GPT-4o mini; Plus (~$20/mo) unlocks GPT-4o, higher limits, and advanced tools.

## How to use it
1. Go to chat.openai.com or install the mobile app and sign up.
2. Type your question in plain language — no special syntax needed.
3. Use the paperclip to attach files, images, or documents for analysis.
4. Start a new chat per topic to keep context clean; use Projects to organize long-running work.

## Prompting tips
- Give context first: 'I'm a data analyst preparing a churn report for executives…' beats a bare question.
- Ask it to show its reasoning for math and logic: 'solve step by step.'
- Iterate: 'make it shorter', 'more formal', 'explain like I'm new to SQL' — treat answers as drafts.
- For code, paste the error message verbatim along with what you expected.

## Gotchas
- Free tier has usage caps that reset every few hours.
- It can confidently state wrong facts — verify anything important.
- Don't paste passwords, private company data, or personal documents you wouldn't share publicly.
