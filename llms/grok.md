# Grok

> xAI's chatbot with real-time access to X (Twitter) posts and web — strongest at 'what's happening right now' questions.

**Category:** Chat / LLMs · **Official site:** https://grok.com

## What it is
xAI's chatbot with real-time access to X (Twitter) posts and web — strongest at 'what's happening right now' questions.

## Best for
Breaking news, social media sentiment, unfiltered answers, X power users.

## Pricing
Free tier with limits; premium X subscriptions raise limits.

## How to use it
1. Use at grok.com or inside the X app.
2. Ask about current events — it sees posts and news as they happen.

## Prompting tips
- Ask 'what are people saying about X on X/Twitter right now?' for live sentiment.
- Fun Mode gives witty answers; default mode is more straightforward.

## Gotchas
- Real-time info can include rumors — treat breaking-news answers as unconfirmed.
- Weaker than Claude/GPT-4o on long-form writing and careful reasoning.
