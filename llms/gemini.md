# Gemini

> Google's AI assistant, deeply integrated with Google Workspace (Gmail, Docs, Drive) and strong at multimodal tasks.

**Category:** Chat / LLMs · **Official site:** https://gemini.google.com

## What it is
Google's AI assistant, deeply integrated with Google Workspace (Gmail, Docs, Drive) and strong at multimodal tasks.

## Best for
Google Workspace users, image understanding, trip planning with Maps data, quick factual lookups.

## Pricing
Free tier; Advanced (~$20/mo, often bundled with Google One) unlocks the most capable model and Workspace integration.

## How to use it
1. Sign in with your Google account at gemini.google.com.
2. Connect Gmail/Drive in settings to let it search your own documents.
3. On Android it can replace Google Assistant for voice queries.

## Prompting tips
- Ask it to pull specifics from your Drive: 'summarize the Q3 report in my Drive.'
- Good at 'compare these two options' questions with a table output: ask for the table explicitly.

## Gotchas
- Workspace-connected answers depend on your files being findable — vague file names hurt results.
- Privacy tradeoff: you're giving Google's AI access to your personal data.
