# Runway

> The leading AI video platform: text-to-video, image-to-video, and a full editing suite.

**Category:** Video generation · **Official site:** https://runwayml.com

## What it is
The leading AI video platform: text-to-video, image-to-video, and a full editing suite. Used by actual film studios.

## Best for
Short AI video clips, image-to-video animation, creative marketing videos, film-style experimentation.

## Pricing
Free tier with limited credits; paid plans from ~$15/mo for serious use.

## How to use it
1. Sign up, pick a model (Gen-3 Alpha is current flagship).
2. Text-to-video: describe the shot like a director ('slow dolly shot, rainy neon street').
3. Image-to-video: upload a still and describe the motion — far more controllable.

## Prompting tips
- Think in shots, not stories: 5-10 second clips, one clear motion each.
- Image-to-video beats text-to-video for consistency — generate the still first, then animate.
- Specify camera movement explicitly: 'static camera', 'slow push-in', 'drone shot'.

## Gotchas
- Credits burn fast; a few seconds of video costs real money.
- Faces and complex motion still glitch — keep motion simple.
