# Sora

> OpenAI's video model — generates up to minute-long, highly coherent video from text.

**Category:** Video generation · **Official site:** https://openai.com/sora

## What it is
OpenAI's video model — generates up to minute-long, highly coherent video from text. Remarkable physics and scene consistency.

## Best for
Longer coherent clips, realistic scene simulation, storyboarding ideas into moving video.

## Pricing
Available to ChatGPT Plus/Pro subscribers; limits vary by tier.

## How to use it
1. Access via sora.chatgpt.com with a Plus/Pro account.
2. Write detailed scene prompts: subject, action, camera, lighting, mood.
3. Use the storyboard feature to chain multiple shots.

## Prompting tips
- Longer prompts with concrete visual detail outperform short ones.
- Remix and recut features let you iterate without starting over.

## Gotchas
- Generation queues can be long at peak times.
- Like all video AI: watch for morphing objects and extra limbs in complex scenes.
