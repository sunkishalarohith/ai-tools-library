# Pika

> A fun, accessible text-to-video tool with playful effects (melt it, explode it, squash it) — big with social media creators.

**Category:** Video generation · **Official site:** https://pika.art

## What it is
A fun, accessible text-to-video tool with playful effects (melt it, explode it, squash it) — big with social media creators.

## Best for
Fun social clips, meme-style effects, quick creative experiments.

## Pricing
Free tier with daily credits; paid plans for more.

## How to use it
1. Sign up at pika.art, type a prompt or upload an image.
2. Try the signature effects: 'melt', 'explode', 'inflate' on any subject.
3. Keep clips short — Pika shines at 3-5 seconds.

## Prompting tips
- Effects work best on a clear single subject against a simple background.
- Pair a Midjourney still with Pika animation for quality + motion.

## Gotchas
- Aimed at fun, not photorealism — don't expect film quality.
- Watermarks on the free tier.
