# Suno

> Type a description, get a full song — vocals, instruments, production — in under a minute.

**Category:** Audio generation · **Official site:** https://suno.com

## What it is
Type a description, get a full song — vocals, instruments, production — in under a minute. Shockingly good.

## Best for
Custom songs, jingles, background music, musical gifts and jokes.

## Pricing
Free tier with daily credits; paid plans from ~$10/mo for more songs and commercial rights.

## How to use it
1. Describe the song: genre, mood, topic — 'upbeat pop song about Monday morning coffee'.
2. Add your own lyrics or let it write them.
3. Generate 2-3 versions and keep the best; extend it for full length.

## Prompting tips
- Genre + mood + one vivid detail beats a long description.
- Custom Mode gives control over lyrics, style tags, and song structure.

## Gotchas
- Free-tier songs are non-commercial — read the license before using in business.
- AI vocals have a recognizable sheen; great for fun, not for fooling musicians.
