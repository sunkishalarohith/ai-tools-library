# ElevenLabs

> The best AI voice generation — eerily human text-to-speech, voice cloning, and dubbing in 29+ languages.

**Category:** Audio generation · **Official site:** https://elevenlabs.io

## What it is
The best AI voice generation — eerily human text-to-speech, voice cloning, and dubbing in 29+ languages.

## Best for
Voiceovers, podcast narration, audiobooks, dubbing videos into other languages.

## Pricing
Free tier (~10k characters/mo); paid plans from ~$5/mo scale up fast.

## How to use it
1. Sign up, pick a premade voice or clone your own (1-3 min of clean audio).
2. Paste text, choose a voice, generate, download the MP3.
3. Use the Dubbing Studio to translate videos with lip-sync-ish timing.

## Prompting tips
- Punctuation directs performance: commas pause, ellipses trail off, ALL CAPS adds emphasis.
- Clone with clean, quiet recordings — background noise clones too.
- Break long scripts into paragraphs and generate separately for best quality.

## Gotchas
- Free tier is tiny — one audiobook chapter can exceed it.
- Voice cloning raises consent issues — only clone voices you have rights to.
